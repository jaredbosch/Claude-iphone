▲ What makes this work — and what breaks it

The loop only closes because Days 2–6 are short. The two 30+ nm crossings (Days 1 and 7) are the weather-critical legs: start them by 08:00 on the morning trades, and check the Kick 'em Jenny alert level (UWI-SRC/NaDMA) before each. If the forecast turns ugly for Day 7, sacrifice Dragon Bay and run Tyrrell → Port Louis direct (33 nm) — same hours, no final-night anchorage.

If Mustique's moorings are full (only ~30 balls, mandatory, book via the Harbour Master ahead): make Day 3 a Mustique day stop — lunch and Basil's — then overnight in Bequia, and take Day 4 as a full Bequia day. Miles barely change.

The overnight spots, ranked by job

Six anchorages over seven nights — two get repeat visits because they're the best-protected stops on the border days.

Nights 1 & 6

Tyrrell Bay, Carriacou

The workhorse: big, all-weather, mud/sand with excellent holding, customs at the marina, fuel and provisions ashore. Anchor ~10 ft on the south side, clear of the ship channel. Las Iguanas & Lambi Queen for dinner.

Shelter: excellentAnchor free · ball ~US$20Clearance stop
Nights 2 & 5

Tobago Cays (behind Horseshoe Reef)

Open to the trades but flat water behind the reef — falling asleep to breeze with nothing between you and Africa. Mooring EC$60/24h + EC$15 pp park fee, paid online. Turtles at Baradel; Romeo's BBQ on Petit Bateau.

Holding: mooringsEC$150/night for 6 · ~EC$180 for 8Breezy — secure the bimini
Night 3 · the prize

Britannia Bay, Mustique

Mandatory mooring (~US$200/3-day permit), anchoring banned. Can roll in a northerly swell — take a ball toward the south end. Basil's Bar is the point of the whole detour; Wednesday is Jump-Up night if your dates line up.

Book ahead — ~30 ballsUS$200 / 3-day permitBest bar of the trip
Night 4

Admiralty Bay, Bequia

The finest anchorage in the chain — huge, well-sheltered, soft mud holding, full town ashore. Ball ~EC$50–60 or anchor off Princess Margaret Beach. This is the reprovision-and-long-dinner night.

Shelter: excellentBall EC$50–60 · anchor freeBest town ashore
Night 6 alternative

Sandy Island, Carriacou

A palm-fringed sandbar in the marine park, 1.5 nm from Tyrrell — take a park mooring for a swimsuit-only evening if the weather is settled. No services; clear in at Tyrrell first, then shift over.

Settled weather onlyPark mooringBest sundowner
Night 7

Dragon Bay, Grenada

Quiet final night on Grenada's lee coast, directly over the Molinere Underwater Sculpture Park — snorkel the statues at golden hour with nobody around, then a 30-minute motor to the base at hand-back.

◆ The honest trade vs the 7-day plan in the dossier

Compared with Option A (which cut Mustique), this loop buys Basil's Bar and a Bequia night by spending the lay day — no zero-mile day, and the Cays get two single nights instead of a full 48 hours. If your crew would rather float than move, Option A is the calmer week; if Mustique is the memory you're chasing, this is how it fits.
